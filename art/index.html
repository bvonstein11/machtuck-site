Art & AI

How our art is made

Last updated 2 November 2026

The Reach: Unknown is ours. Its world, its five factions, their machines and their story are written and designed by our team.

Where AI comes in

We use AI image tools for some of the pictures on this site. Every one starts from our own designs and descriptions, and our team directs it, chooses it and approves it before it appears here. We say so plainly, because we never present an AI-generated image as hand-made.

Respecting other creators

Our machines and factions are original designs. We screen our images for resemblance to other studios' work, and we change anything that comes too close.

If you believe something on this site uses your work without permission, email support@machtuck.com with a link to it and to your work. We will look into it promptly and act on what we find.
