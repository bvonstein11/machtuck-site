Privacy

This site collects only what you send us

Last updated 5 November 2026

machtuck.com is run by Mach Tuck Games LLC. It has no accounts, and we keep no record of your visit. The only personal information it can collect is what you type into our closed beta form, and only if you send it.

No cookies

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, so there is nothing to accept or decline.

No analytics or tracking

We run no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we do not use any service that follows you across sites. Our fonts and pictures are served from this site itself, so your visit is not reported to a font or image service.

The closed beta form

If you send the form, these go to Kit, the email service we use, and are kept on our list there:

  • your email address;
  • the callsign you request, which is a request and not a reservation;
  • your faction, if you choose one (it is optional);
  • where you came from, if the link you followed says so. Some of our links name their source, such as ?src=tiktok for a link from TikTok. We read that from the address only at the moment you send the form, and it is never stored in your browser.

You must tick a box to say you are 13 or older before the form will send. The tick itself is not stored.

What we use it for

To tell you when closed beta invitations go out, to send you news about The Reach, and to plan callsigns and factions for the closed beta. We never sell your details. We share them with no one except Kit, which holds them for us, unless the law requires it.

Kit

Sending the form takes you through Kit's site for a moment and brings you back here. Kit handles your details under its own privacy policy. Like most email services, it may record technical details of the sign-up, such as the IP address it came from, and whether an email is opened and which of its links are clicked.

Unsubscribing and deletion

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. To see what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email support@machtuck.com. We keep your details until you ask us to delete them or we no longer run the list.

Children

This site and its form are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us their details, email support@machtuck.com and we will delete them.

Our host

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub keeps standard server logs, including the IP address of each visitor, to run and protect its service. We do not see or receive those logs. GitHub explains what it keeps in its Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Links on this site, such as our community on Discord, take you to services with their own privacy policies. What you share there is governed by them.

The game

The Reach: Unknown has its own privacy policy, which covers what the game needs to run. It will be published here before the closed beta opens.

Changes and questions

If this changes, we will update this page and its date first. Questions: support@machtuck.com.
