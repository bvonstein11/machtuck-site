Privacy

This site collects nothing about you

Last updated 2 November 2026

machtuck.com is run by Mach Tuck Games LLC. It has no accounts, no forms and no sign-up, and we keep no record of your visit.

No cookies

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, so there is nothing to accept or decline.

No analytics or tracking

We run no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we do not use any service that follows you across sites. Our fonts and pictures are served from this site itself, so your visit is not reported to a font or image service.

Our host

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub keeps standard server logs, including the IP address of each visitor, to run and protect its service. We do not see or receive those logs. GitHub explains what it keeps in its Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Links on this site, such as our community on Discord, take you to services with their own privacy policies. What you share there is governed by them.

The game

The Reach: Unknown has its own privacy policy, which covers what the game needs to run. It will be published here before the closed beta opens.

Changes and questions

If this changes, we will update this page and its date first. Questions: support@machtuck.com.
