The Game

Your victories matter beyond the match.

You are a mech pilot. You have a faction. You have a rank. You have people who depend on you. Your reputation outlasts any single session.

The Reach: Unknown is a persistent mech war. Join one of five factions, or fly alone as a Torrath mercenary, and fight for ground on a contested world. Every match feeds a war that never ends. The community is the story.

  1. 01

    The clan is the game

    Up to fifty pilots under one name. You fly for them, and they remember who showed up.

  2. 02

    Consequence matters

    Wins and losses move the line on the map. The war keeps the score.

  3. 03

    Honor is mechanical

    How you fight counts, not only whether you win.

  4. 04

    Weight and permanence

    Mechs have mass, loadouts have a price, and the map never resets.

  5. 05

    The universe never finishes

    Most of The Reach is unmapped. Every season the front pushes into new ground.

The Reach

The nearest star to Earth, and an Earth-like world in its light.

The Archive is bound for it on a 32.63-year voyage: the first home humanity has found since Earth failed. Every faction aboard wants to claim it. Nobody has agreed on who has the right.
