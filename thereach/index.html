The story so far

How we got here.

Earth was failing. The probes found one world worth the trip, and a small crew went ahead to build.

For years the settlement's logs came home on schedule. The last arrived in June 3340. Then the signal stopped.

Now The Archive is still coming. Nobody aboard agrees on what the silence means, and when we reach orbit, we will find out.

    Aboard · Five factions

    Nobody was born into a faction. Every member chose.

    Five answers to why Earth failed. Pick one.

    Honor

    Kashiro

    “We failed because we let small things go. Precision is honor.”

    Honor that costs something real. They fly as a disciplined team and settle disputes by trial of combat.

    Dominion

    Valdron

    “We failed because we were divided. Unity is survival.”

    Humanity needs one strong leader, they believe. They advance in overwhelming force and hold what they take.

    Shadow

    Darvyn

    “We failed because we believed what we were told. Information is the only honest currency.”

    Realists who trust data over intentions. They would rather win before the battle starts.

    Pack

    Gorvik

    “We failed because we each looked after our own. The pack eats first.”

    Tribalists who trust only the pilot beside them. Fiercely loyal inside the pack.

    Foundation

    Shenvong

    “We failed because we never built anything that could hold. What holds, saves.”

    Builders. They fight to make fighting unnecessary, and measure it by what still stands after they are gone.

    Or swear to none of them. Decline all five and you fly as : a mercenary, and free.
