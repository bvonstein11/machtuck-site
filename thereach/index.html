The Archive

Built to remember.

The story so far

How we got here.

  1. Aug 3289

    Earth was failing.

    There was no saving it, so there was a plan: send probes to the nearest stars at half the speed of light, and find a new home.

  2. Mar 3304 · The first answer

    A world, with water.

    Alpha Centauri, the closest star, more than four light-years away. Its report took more than four years to reach us, and Earth could not wait for a second.

  3. The chosen

    20,000 people.

    Picked for what they could do, to make the crossing on the Archive.

  4. 3308 · The advance crew

    A small ship went first.

    A few hundred crew left two years ahead of the Archive, on a faster ship, to make a place for the rest of us.

  5. 19 Jan 3310

    The Archive left Earth.

    Families came aboard with young children. It carried everything worth saving, and the memory of what went wrong.

  6. More than thirty years

    We crossed awake.

    Children were born aboard who would never see Earth, and every deck trained for the work waiting on the ground.

  7. Aboard the Archive

    Five mission groups.

    Each worked its own deck, called by the name of its department head. Those five names are the factions now.

    GroundworksValdron deck
    HabitatShenvong deck
    SurveyKashiro deck
    Forward ReconDarvyn deck
    General LabourGorvik deck

  8. Sent home from The Reach

    Jul 3325 · The Reach

    The advance crew landed.

    Seventeen years after they left Earth, they came down on open ground, with nothing built.

  9. Sent home from The Reach

    Jul 3328 · The Reach

    They switched on the signal.

    Three years on, they knew it could be done, and the signal told the Archive they had made it. It reached us in 3330, and every deck had hope.

  10. Sent home from The Reach

    3330 to 3337 · The Reach

    Year by year, it grew.

    Every picture they sent showed more: shelters, then domes, then fields.

  11. Sent home from The Reach

    Oct 3330 to Mar 3337

    We sent help ahead.

    A faster ship left the Archive with new materials, and young people to help and to learn. Children were born there, and every year we flew closer.

  12. Jun 3340

    Then the signal stopped.

    The logs didn't come anymore. Deck by deck, no one could agree on what it meant. That is when the fracture began.

  13. Now

    The Archive is still coming.

    Nobody aboard knows what is waiting on the ground, or who will lead when we land.

Aboard · Five factions

The ship split along its decks.

When the signal stopped, each mission group closed ranks on its own deck, still called by the name of the department head Earth gave it. Every deck has its own answer to why Earth failed, and its own plan for the ground. Nobody was born into one; every member chose. And down in the bays, the building has already started.

Five answers to why Earth failed. Pick one.

Kashiro deck · Survey

Honor that costs something real, chosen rather than inherited.

When we land
Land in order and fly as one disciplined team under a strict chain of command. Disputes are settled by trial of combat.
In the bays
Kashiro Engineering Division is already building: precise, nothing decorative, nothing wasted. Built the way they fight.

Valdron deck · Groundworks

Humanity needs one strong leader, they believe. Not cruel: convinced.

When we land
Come down in overwhelming force, machines and crews together, and hold every metre they take.
In the bays
Valdron Heavy Industries builds overbuilt and imposing, to project certainty. The advance never stops.

Darvyn deck · Forward Recon

Realists who trust data over intentions.

When we land
Know the ground before anyone lands, then drop where nobody is looking. They would rather win before the battle starts.
In the bays
Darvyn Systems Collective builds for deception: no markings, weapons flush, one cold light.

Gorvik deck · General Labour, “the Pool”

Tribalists who trust only the pilot beside them. Fiercely loyal inside the pack.

When we land
Small packs and fast raids, back into cover before anyone can answer.
In the bays
Gorvik Fabrication builds from whatever there is, and no two come out alike. Every part is there to bring the pilot home.

Shenvong deck · Habitat

Builders. They fight to make fighting unnecessary.

When we land
Dig in first, build walls that hold, and outlast everyone. They measure it by what still stands after they are gone.
In the bays
Shenvong Industrial Works builds the heaviest armour and the best cooling aboard, for the war after this one.

Snow Highlands

High, cold country: a valley of snowfield and pine inside a ring of peaks.

The ground rolls in long white hills, open between the stands of pine, with rock breaking through on the steep sides. You can see a long way up here, and so can everyone else.

Arid Desert / Mesa

Rust-red sand and cliffs under a hard sun, with steam rising off the desert floor.

Open ground rolls out between the cliffs, with vents steaming through pale grey crust and boulders strewn across the floor, and the sightlines go on for a mile. When the wind rises, a dust storm closes all of it down to about a hundred yards.

Pilot briefing · The game

Every match moves the war.

You are a mech pilot with a faction, a rank and a clan that counts on you. What you do tonight is still on the map tomorrow.

Every pilot fights on the same front line.

Rank · Twenty-two steps

Rank is earned. A commission is accepted.

Eleven enlisted ranks, Private to Sergeant Major. Eleven officer ranks, Second Lieutenant to General of the Faction. Promotion takes time served, hours flown and damage done, all three together. Rank carries on through every season, and the one way down is killing your own side.

The commission

Sergeant Major is the top of the enlisted ranks, and the next step is a question. Keep serving until you've earned Second Lieutenant, and the game asks whether you want your commission.

Nobody can hand it to you. You accept by typing the words yourself. Defer and you stay a Sergeant Major, your service still counting, and the offer waits under Settings for as long as you like.

Congratulations

You've earned a commission as an officer.

Sergeant Major → to Second Lieutenant

Would you like to accept your commission?

If you do not accept, you will retain the rank of Sergeant Major.

Commission deferred

You remain a Sergeant Major, and your service keeps counting.

You can accept your commission at any time under Settings.

  3. 03

    Rank and Valor

    Ribbons record how you fight, and your rank carries from season to season.

    Valor is won in Ranked, one on one, best of three. A 2–0 win is +200 and a 2–1 win is +100. A lost series costs the same.

    Every wing is a floor. Once you earn one, a bad run this season can't take it from you.

    Each season starts again at Rookie. The best wings you ever earned stay on your record.

  4. 04

    Clans

    A clan holds up to fifty pilots under one banner, and they remember who showed up.

    Design your clan's patch. Every piece and every stitch is the game's own. Keep the code: when you can found a clan, it brings this patch into the game.
