PRIVACY POLICY - THE REACH: UNKNOWN (CLOSED BETA)

Effective 10 November 2026

This policy explains what Mach Tuck Games LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("we", "us"), collects when you play THE REACH: UNKNOWN (the "Game"), why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted. Our website has its own policy at machtuck.com/privacy, which covers the closed beta form.

1. What the Game collects

Always, to run the Game:

  • Account ids: an anonymous player id from Unity Gaming Services, an id Unity's services create for your installation, and an id the Game creates on your device. Once Steam sign-in is on, also your Steam ID and its link to your account.
  • Your career: callsign and tag, faction, clan and patch, rank, Valor, statistics, Marks, Chits, salvage, gear and Tier One status, plus the game choices saved with it: the mech you last picked and its loadouts, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your answer to a commission. Your other settings stay on your device (below).
  • Your mercenary record: contracts, the house you fly for, your reputation and recent form.
  • A record of each online match, under your player id: mode, times, team, result, score and deaths, damage dealt and taken, and the gear you fielded.
  • A hidden skill rating for matchmaking, by mech class, with your games, wins, losses and draws. Other players never see it.
  • Connection data: the match server receives your IP address, and writes it to that server's log when you join.
  • Your acceptance of these documents: the version you accepted and the time our server received it.

In the closed beta:

  • Your tester number. The match server links it to your IP address in its log, and we keep a lasting record of which player ids used it, with when they were first and last seen and on which server. We use this to let only invited testers in, to shut out a leaked token, and to find your account if you lose it.
  • A test-group tag. Pilots of our invited alpha nights are tagged "alpha-nights", with the matches counted and whether they fell before or after the pilot's first upgrade. We use it to measure balance, not to rate you. You cannot switch it off, and a tag stays once set. It holds no gear.

Only if you do something:

  • Beta reports, when you send one: your words and category, a screenshot of the game (which can show other pilots' callsigns and chat), your callsign and player id, the build, your operating system, device model, processor, memory, graphics card, screen and frame rate, where you are in the game, the match and server you are on, your local time and time zone, your last match, and the game's last 30 warnings and errors.
  • Clan chat, when you send it: each line with your callsign, player id and time. A clan keeps its latest 200 lines. Only its members can read them in the game; we can read them to moderate.
  • Drop-room and match chat passes through the match server to the other pilots. The Game does not store it. A filter checks it, and you can mute, block and report.
  • Support email and recovery: if you write to support@machtuck.com we receive your address and message. To prove an account is yours we may ask for your callsign, Steam profile, Discord name and when you first played.

Telemetry: the Game writes a log of what happens in matches and menus on your device from the first launch, up to its latest 8,000 events, under a session id. It leaves your device only if you tick the optional box, at the start or in Settings. Once ticked, the log already on your device is sent too. It is then stored under your player id. Unticking stops further sending; to delete what was sent, write to us. Separately, match servers send us their own balance figures for each match (such as gear spread and damage by zone). These identify a mech in that match, not an account by name, but can be matched to the match record. They do not depend on the box.

Recovery and moderation records: if we move or restore your career, including carrying a career from your device into your server wallet, we log who did it, when, the accounts involved, a note, and a copy of what was overwritten so it can be undone. That copy is removed after 90 days, or when you have your account deleted. If we ban you or change a name, we keep the reason, the staff member, the dates and the notice to you.

Kept only on your device: your age check result (passed or refused, never your birth date), your telemetry choice, your settings (controls, units, display, on-screen options, subtitles and motion), and the Game's log file. We do not receive these. The Game has no automatic crash reporter.

Test builds only: a test build that says it records may log how you fly (movement, aiming, jets, range of each shot) to teach our computer pilots to play more like people. Normal builds do not.

The Game does not collect your real name, phone number or payment details. Purchases, once offered, are made through Steam, which handles payment. Email reaches us only if you use our website's form or write to us; if you sign up on the site, Kit holds your address as machtuck.com/privacy explains.

What other pilots see: your callsign and tag, faction, clan and patch, rank, Valor wings, leaderboard standing, your part in the war, your chat, and what you do in a match. The weekly war report, when it runs, names leading pilots and clans by callsign and clan name in a public Discord channel. If you ask, we leave you out of it.

2. Who receives it

We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising. These companies run parts of the Game for us under their own terms:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services): sign-in, cloud saves, server logic, and all stored data above.
  • Edgegap: hosts match servers, which receive your IP address and connection data.
  • Discord: a private channel tells us a beta report arrived, with its number, its category and the game's build. The report itself stays with Unity. Discord also carries the public war report. If you join our Discord, Discord's own policy applies.
  • Anthropic (Claude): we review beta reports, including the screenshot and context, with AI tools.
  • Valve (Steam): distributes the Game and, once on, signs you in.
  • Kit: holds the email list of people who sign up on our website.
  • ImprovMX and our email provider: carry mail sent to support@machtuck.com.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect players or the Game.

3. How long we keep it

  • Account, career and mercenary record, rating, tester pairing and test-group tag: while your account exists, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Match records, telemetry and beta reports: for the beta and up to 12 months after it.
  • Clan chat: the latest 200 lines, while the clan exists.
  • IP addresses in match server logs: for the life of that match server, plus any log retention at our host.
  • Ban and moderation records, and recovery logs: as long as needed to enforce them and protect the Game, even after your other data is deleted.
  • Support email: as long as needed to answer you, then deleted on request.

4. Your rights, and how to delete your data

Wherever you live, you can ask us to see, export, correct or delete your data. Write to support@machtuck.com. We do this by hand, so we will first check the account is yours, and we answer within 30 days. Deleting your account ends your career; it cannot be undone. Copies already shared, such as a public war report, may remain where we can't remove them. You can withdraw telemetry consent at any time in Settings.

If you are in the EU, the UK, California, Colorado or another place with privacy law, you also have the rights that law gives you, including complaining to your regulator. We process telemetry on your consent, and the rest to provide the Game you asked to play and for our legitimate interest in keeping it fair and safe.

5. Children and teenagers

The Game is not for children under 13. An age check refuses anyone younger, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, write to us and we will delete it. If you are 13 to 17, a parent or guardian must agree before you play. A parent or guardian may ask us to see or delete a teenager's data.

6. Security

Sign-in has no password to steal: today it is anonymous, through Unity's services. Data is stored with the providers above, and the Game's connections to Unity's services are encrypted. Connections to a match server are not encrypted. Only our staff list can read stored reports and telemetry, and we log restores, bans and moderation. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you of a breach as the law requires.

7. International transfers

We and our providers may process your data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires, transfers rely on recognized safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Changes

We will post changes at machtuck.com/thereach/privacy, and the Game will ask you to accept a changed version before you keep playing.
