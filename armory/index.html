Sixty-five rounds a second. One barely marks the paint; the stream takes a mech apart. Fire it in bursts.

Heat is the other magazine. At 100 the VIGIL shuts down. It sheds heat fastest standing still, slower on the move, and flat out it builds. The heavier your fit, the less it sheds, so the cooling here is the fit you chose up top.

The arithmetic

Standing sheds 13.5 heat a second, walking 6, each times your fit's cooling. Flat out, in the top fifth of the speed range, sheds nothing and builds more the faster you run.

At 100 the VIGIL brakes to a stop, up to 5 seconds, then heat falls to 50 over 5 seconds. The guns come back under 60, 4 seconds into that fall.

Damage counts every round landing. In a fight the wind, the drop and your aim take their share: The Physics flies them.

Every 500 damage is one part: 70% the part you hit, 30% the wildcard. No roll, no luck.

Arms pay the parts of the guns riding them, and this VIGIL carries the fit you chose up top. Side torsos pay Coolant Cells. The centre torso pays Armor Plating from the front and Focusing Optics from behind. Legs and the pelvis pay nothing.

The arithmetic

A centre-torso hit on a VIGIL with a module fitted pays half to the module's own part. The shoulder pod rides the right arm.

The wildcard is Power Cells, and the battlefield turns a third of it into its own part: Armor Plating and Warhead Casings in snow, Focusing Optics in desert, Servo Actuators in jungle, Coolant Cells on volcanic ground. The total never changes.

  • Autoloader Parts build PULSE-50 and CHAIN-30 upgrades.
  • Focusing Optics build DART-20 upgrades.
  • Warhead Casings build the other five weapons' upgrades.
  • Armor Plating buys the VIGIL's armour and CT armor.
  • Servo Actuators buy the VIGIL's speed and overboost.
  • Coolant Cells buy the VIGIL's cooling and reactor cooling.
  • Airframe Members buy jump jets.
  • Power Cells stand in for any part you are short of, two cells for one part.
