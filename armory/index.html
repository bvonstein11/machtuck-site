Six guns, three hardpoints

The right arm carries one, the left arm two. Fit any three that are yours, and switch between them in the fight: managing heat across your guns is part of the skill.

Shoulder · Ordnance

One bay, two choices

The shoulder takes one piece of ordnance per drop. The rocket pod is where every VIGIL starts; the mortar takes its place.

ShoulderRocket pod

VOLLEY-6

Every pilot starts with it

Eighteen tubes, fired six at a time. A salvo ripples out of the pod in under half a second and spreads in a tight cone: 12 damage a rocket, 72 if all six land, and the farther the target, the more of them miss. Dumb-fire, no lock; you aim it. Three salvos six seconds apart empty the pod, and its one reload takes twelve.

Heat

Heat is the other magazine

Every weapon here makes heat, and at 100 the VIGIL shuts down. Heat bleeds away fastest when you stand still, slower on the move, and not at all at a run, and a heavier VIGIL sheds less of it. So a fight has a rhythm: fire, move, cool, and switch to a cooler gun before the needle gets there.

Salvage

You salvage what you destroy

Every point of damage you put into an enemy's arms and torsos comes home as parts, and parts pay for upgrades. There is no roll: the more you break, the more you bring back.

  • Arms pay the parts of the weapons riding them: the shoulder pod rides the right arm.
  • Side torsos pay Coolant Cells.
  • The centre torso pays Armor Plating from the front and Focusing Optics from behind.
  • A fitted module adds its own part to a centre-torso hit.
  • ×Legs and the pelvis pay nothing.

The wildcard is Power Cells, which stand in for any part, with a share turned into the battlefield's own part.

The daily crate

Once a day a salvage crate fills the gaps with Power Cells, graded one to five. A strong match raises your odds of the top grades.
