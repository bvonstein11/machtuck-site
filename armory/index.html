The Armory

Shoulder · Ordnance

One bay, two choices

The shoulder takes one piece of ordnance per drop. The rocket pod is where every VIGIL starts; the mortar takes its place.

ShoulderRocket pod

VOLLEY-6

Every pilot starts with it

Eighteen tubes, fired six at a time. A salvo ripples out of the pod in under half a second and spreads in a tight cone: 12 damage a rocket, 72 if all six land, and the farther the target, the more of them miss. Dumb-fire, no lock; you aim it. Three salvos six seconds apart empty the pod, and its one reload takes twelve.

Centre torso · Modules

The fifth slot

Heat

Heat is the other magazine

Every weapon here makes heat, and at 100 the VIGIL shuts down. Heat bleeds away fastest when you stand still, slower on the move, and not at all at a run, and a heavier VIGIL sheds less of it. So a fight has a rhythm: fire, move, cool, and switch to a cooler gun before the needle gets there.

Marks

Earned, then improved

Yours from the start

PULSE-50, CHAIN-30, SLUG-250 and VOLLEY-6 come with every VIGIL. Together they are a full fighting loadout.

Earned in the fight

DART-20, THUMP-82, SPIKE-90 and BREACH-275 cost 25,000 Marks each, and Marks are earned by playing. Earn one once and it is yours on any VIGIL you fly.

Mk I to Mk IV

Every weapon climbs from Mk I to Mk IV. You qualify for each step by flying it, then pay for the step in Marks. What a step improves depends on the gun: the PULSE-50 and CHAIN-30 run cooler, the DART-20 reaches farther, and the rest hit harder.
