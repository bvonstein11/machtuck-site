Playing fair in the game

You must be 13 or older to play The Reach: Unknown.

Friendly fire. In Proving Grounds and The Front, destroying a teammate's mech (a teamkill) has consequences. Field Exercise is never affected.

  • One warning. The first match in which you teamkill costs you nothing but a warning, however many teammates go down. You get that warning once, ever.
  • Then one rank a match. After the warning, any match in which you teamkill costs you one rank, never more than one per match.
  • Time-outs. At the lowest rank, or after a third teamkill match within 90 days, you are timed out of Proving Grounds and The Front: 24 hours the first time, then 72 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 60 days and 90 days. Field Exercise stays open while you wait.
  • Removed from the match. Two teamkills in one match remove you from it, and an autopilot takes your seat.
  • A clean record. 90 days with no teamkill resets the time-out ladder. The warning does not come back.
  • Review. A teamkill after a 90-day time-out sends your account to our staff, who may remove it from the game for good. A person makes that call, never a machine.

One pilot, one account. Using a second account, or another player's, to win rank, Valor or rewards for yourself or a friend is not allowed. We review pairs of accounts that trade lopsided wins, and we may take back what was won that way.
