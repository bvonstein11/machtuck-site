Short description

The Reach: Unknown is a persistent, first-person mech war. Pilots join one of five factions, or fly alone as a Torrath mercenary, and fight for ground on The Reach, the first home humanity has found since Earth failed. Every win and every loss shifts a front line that all pilots share and that never resets, and clans of up to fifty pilots hold what they take.

The setting

Earth was failing. An evacuation sent a probe to the nearest star, then a small crew to go ahead, then the Archive: a moving city of about twenty thousand people on a crossing of 32.63 years. The crew sent ahead landed, built a settlement and called home. Then the signal stopped. Five factions were born aboard during the crossing, each with its own answer to why Earth failed, and each means to claim the new world.

Key features

  • Fly for a clan. Up to fifty pilots under one banner, who remember who showed up.
  • Move the front. Every win and every loss shifts it on a map all pilots share.
  • Honor counts. How you fight counts, not only whether you win, and your rank carries from season to season.
  • Feel the weight. Every fight is first person, from a full cockpit. Mechs have mass, and every loadout has a price.
