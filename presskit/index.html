Descriptions

One line

A persistent mech war: join one of five factions or fly alone as a Torrath mercenary, and every match you fight moves a front all pilots share.

Short

The Reach: Unknown is a persistent, first-person mech war. Pilots join one of five factions, or fly alone as a Torrath mercenary, and fight for ground on The Reach, the first home humanity has found since Earth failed. Every win and every loss shifts a front line that all pilots share, and clans of up to fifty pilots hold what they take.

Long

Earth was failing. An evacuation sent a probe to the nearest star, then a small crew to go ahead, then the Archive: a moving city of about twenty thousand people on a crossing of 32.63 years. The crew sent ahead landed on The Reach, built a settlement and called home. Then the logs stopped coming. Five factions were born aboard during the crossing, each with its own answer to why Earth failed, and each means to claim the new world.

The Reach: Unknown is the war that follows, fought from a full first-person cockpit. Mechs have mass, and every loadout has a price. Pilots join one of five factions or fly alone as a Torrath mercenary, and every win and every loss shifts a front line that all of them share. Clans of up to fifty pilots hold what they take. How you fight counts, not only whether you win, and your rank carries from season to season.

The game is in development at Mach Tuck Games, an independent studio, for Windows and Mac on Steam, with iOS and Android to follow. One world, one war, built to run for years.

Features

  • Move the front. Every win and every loss shifts it on a map all pilots share. The Front opens as its own event after the closed beta begins.
  • Feel the weight. Every fight is first person, from a full cockpit. Mechs have mass, and every loadout has a price.
  • Honor counts. How you fight counts, not only whether you win, and your rank carries from season to season.
  • Fly for a clan. Up to fifty pilots under one banner, who remember who showed up.
  • Five factions, or none. Five decks of the Archive, each with its own answer to why Earth failed, or fly alone as a Torrath mercenary.
  • A world built to last. One world, one war, built to run for years, in snow, jungle, desert and volcanic badlands.
